Responsible AI practices

  • Privacy: Use fictional or anonymised examples. Do not paste passwords, customer records, financial details, or confidential company information.
  • Human oversight: Review and correct all AI-generated emails and summaries before sharing.
  • Accuracy: Check names, facts, deadlines, tone, and calculations. AI can make mistakes.
  • Transparency: Explain when AI assists with a work product if workplace policy requires it.
  • Fairness: Avoid using AI output alone to make consequential decisions about employees or customers.
  • Security: Task data is stored in this browser's local storage. Avoid using this prototype with sensitive information, especially on shared computers.
